## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Find out how ready you are for Cyber Essentials in around two hours — a guided readiness assessment for UK SMEs at £199 instead of £2,000–5,000 in consultancy.
**What it does:** BrightCert walks UK SMEs through a plain-English, 60-question assessment across the five official Cyber Essentials control areas, uses AI analysis to produce a readiness score and gap findings, and generates a prioritised remediation report as a PDF. The assessment is free to complete; the full report unlocks at £199.
**Product category:** Cyber Essentials readiness / compliance preparation software (customers search: "Cyber Essentials checklist", "Cyber Essentials cost", "how to prepare for Cyber Essentials", "Cyber Essentials self assessment").
**Product type:** Self-serve SaaS (one-time purchase + subscriptions), live at brightcert.co.uk.
**Business model:** Assessment £199 one-time (free to complete, pay to unlock report) — **live founding offer: £99 including VAT with the existing £100 FOUNDING10 discount from £199, for the first 10 customers**, visible on the homepage, pricing page, results paywall, sidebar CTA, and the unlock-reminder email. ## Target Audience
**Target companies:** UK SMEs (micro to medium, 1–249 staff), any sector, preparing for Cyber Essentials — typically because a customer, contract, tender, or insurer requires it. Secondary: UK MSPs/IT providers serving those SMEs.
**Decision-makers:** Owner/MD at micro-small; operations manager or office manager at small-medium; the MSP account manager where IT is outsourced.
**Primary use case:** "Are we ready for Cyber Essentials, and what do we need to fix before we apply?"
**Jobs to be done:**
- Find out where we stand without paying a consultant first
- Turn auditor-speak requirements into a plain-English fix list the team can act on
- Produce something structured (PDF) to hand to the IT provider / board / bidding team
**Use cases:** pre-tender scramble (CE required to bid), insurer/customer requirement, MSP standardising CE prep across clients, annual renewal prep.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** SMEs know Cyber Essentials matters but can't tell how close they are to passing, and the requirements are written for auditors, not business owners.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Consultants: £2,000–5,000 and you pay before you know your gaps
- The free NCSC/IASME readiness questions: static, generic, no scoring, no prioritised fix list, no artefact to share
- Just applying: ~£320+VAT per attempt, failure wastes weeks and morale
**What it costs them:** delayed or lost tenders, consultancy spend before understanding gaps, weeks of internal back-and-forth.
**Emotional tension:** fear of failing the real assessment, embarrassment at not knowing "basic" security answers, dread of a long technical project.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** CE readiness/compliance tools (e.g. CyberSmart, Cynomi for MSPs) — priced and packaged for larger orgs or annual contracts, not a £199 one-off answer.
**Secondary:** Cyber Essentials consultants — thorough but £2,000–5,000, slow, and paid before gaps are known.
**Indirect:** IASME's free pre-application questions / DIY spreadsheets — free but no analysis, no prioritisation, no report; and "just apply and see" — expensive failure loop.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Free to complete, pay only to unlock the report (see value before spending)
- Plain-English questions and findings, written for owners not auditors
- AI-generated, prioritised (P1/P2/P3) remediation plan, not a generic checklist
- Honest scope: readiness prep only, no fake certification promises
- UK-specific end to end (language, pricing, NCSC/IASME context)
**Why customers choose us:** cheapest credible way to answer "are we ready?" in an afternoon, with an artefact they can hand to whoever fixes things.

## Customer Language
**How they describe the problem (assumed, to validate with first customers):**
- "We need Cyber Essentials for a contract and I don't know where to start"
- "The questions don't make sense to me, I'm not technical"

**Words to use:** ready / readiness, plain English, what to fix first, before you apply, pass, gap, checklist (as the thing we're better than).
**Words to avoid:** "certification" as something we issue (legal line: we prepare, IASME-licensed Certification Bodies certify); guarantee/promise of passing; alarmist security fear-mongering; em-dashes in visible copy (brand rule).

|------|---------|
