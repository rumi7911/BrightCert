# BrightCert social carousel redesign

Status: Approved direction, two-slide proof pending

Owner: Muhammad Sohaib Roomi

Design surface: Canva

Format: 1080 x 1350 px, 4:5

## Goal

Replace the rejected text-heavy PDF with a distinctive, mobile-readable social
carousel that makes the official Cyber Essentials fee bands immediately useful
to UK SME owners.

The first implementation is limited to two high-fidelity proof slides:

1. Cover
2. Official fee-band comparison

The remaining five slides must not be designed until the owner approves both
proof slides.

## Approved visual direction

Use the **Editorial Authority** direction, interpreted as a contemporary
business publication rather than a sparse report template.

The design must use:

- strong typographic composition that fills the canvas intentionally;
- BrightCert navy `#0F2044`, emerald `#047857`, warm off-white `#F3F4EC` and
  white;
- one expressive editorial serif for key numbers and headlines;
- Inter for supporting copy and JetBrains Mono for labels and source lines;
- the real BrightCert logo mark;
- thin rules, folio numbers and compact source labels;
- visual devices that explain the information, not decorative cyber imagery.

Do not use:

- generic stock cyber imagery, hackers, padlocks, shields or code backgrounds;
- dashboard cards, neon gradients or excessive rounded rectangles;
- large unused areas that do not strengthen hierarchy;
- body text that becomes unreadable at phone size;
- inconsistent serif weights or default office-document typography.

## Proof slide 1: cover

Purpose: stop the scroll and communicate the useful answer immediately.

Content:

- Label: `CYBER ESSENTIALS COST · UK 2026`
- Main typographic graphic: `£320` and `£600 + VAT`
- Connecting copy: `Four official fee bands. One depends on your team size.`
- Supporting line: `Plus the two costs most budgets miss.`
- Folio: `01 / 07`
- Footer: `BrightCert · UK SME Budgeting Series`

Composition:

- Navy base.
- The two prices are oversized graphic elements, not a conventional heading.
- Use an emerald connector, rule or directional device between the prices.
- Keep the supporting copy visibly secondary but readable on a phone.
- Show the BrightCert mark once, with enough clear space to remain recognisable.
- Avoid a button or CTA on the cover.

## Proof slide 2: fee-band comparison

Purpose: answer the fee question in one saved or shared image.

Content:

- Label: `OFFICIAL FEE STRUCTURE`
- Headline: `Find your fee band`
- Four verified tiers:
  - `0 to 9 employees · £320 + VAT`
  - `10 to 49 employees · £440 + VAT`
  - `50 to 249 employees · £500 + VAT`
  - `250 or more employees · £600 + VAT`
- Source: `IASME`
- Reviewed: `29 July 2026`
- Folio: `02 / 07`

Composition:

- Warm off-white base.
- Use a rising four-step editorial diagram, not a plain spreadsheet table.
- Employee ranges progress left-to-right or bottom-to-top.
- Prices remain the dominant scan path.
- Use emerald to indicate progression and navy for primary type.
- The source and reviewed date must be visible without competing with the data.

## Content and compliance

- Use UK English.
- Use `+ VAT` wherever the fee is shown.
- Do not imply BrightCert issues certification or guarantees a pass.
- Do not reproduce official assessment questions.
- Do not use an em dash in visible copy.
- Treat IASME as the source for the four published fee bands.

## Acceptance

The proof is ready for owner review only when:

- both slides are exactly 1080 x 1350 px;
- the cover remains legible as a small thumbnail;
- all four fee bands can be read on a phone without zooming;
- the BrightCert mark is sharp and proportionate;
- spacing is intentional and no text is clipped or crowded;
- the visual system is recognisably different from the rejected NotebookLM PDF;
- a preview of both slides is shown before the remaining five are produced.

## Out of scope

- Designing slides 3 to 7.
- Publishing or scheduling social posts.
- Replacing the previously committed master deck.
- Changing website content, application code or production services.
