### Sourcing

The five test cases come from the **published NCSC Cyber Essentials Plus test
specification**, in the order the specification gives them, including Test case
2's pass criteria: vendor critical/high, **or CVSS v3 base score 7 or above**,
or no vendor detail — failing where a fix has been available for **more than 14
days**. Sampling rules, the cloud-account rule (at least one standard and one
administrative user per service) and the observed-user structure of test cases
3, 4 and 5 are from the same document.

The **April 2026 changes** are from IASME's own update notice:

- a retest rechecks the original sample **and a new random sample**, and a
  second failure means certification is not awarded;
- the verified self-assessment must be finalised and unchanged before Plus
  testing begins.

Neither appears on our existing `ce-vs-ce-plus` hub page, and both are recent
enough that most competing pages do not carry them. They are the reason the
article has something to say rather than restating the comparison.

The three-month limit between Cyber Essentials and the Plus audit is confirmed
against IASME guidance.

**Fetching problems worth recording:** `iasme.co.uk` returns 403 to automated
