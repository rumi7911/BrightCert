## What was written

`How to prepare for a Cyber Essentials Plus assessment` — 552 lines, targeting
`cyber essentials plus assessment` (16 impressions, position 65.1).

The editorial decision worth recording is the **shape**. The obvious version of
this article is another control-by-control checklist, which would have
duplicated both the week 4 checklist and the week 6 audit walkthrough. Instead
it is a **four-week countdown**, and the justification comes from the primary
sources rather than from style: two of the constraints are time windows, not
settings.

- The 14-day patching rule means the fortnight before the audit is the period
  actually being assessed. That is a calendar fact, not a technical one.
- Unsupported software has no same-week remedy, because no fix exists to apply.
  Replacement or scope change is measured in weeks, so it has to be found four
  weeks out.

Everything else — accounts, credentials, availability — is fast to do and easy
to forget, so it sits in the final week. The table carries blank **owner** and
**status** columns in the same shape as the week 4 readiness checklist, and the
page has print styles so it can be used rather than only read.
