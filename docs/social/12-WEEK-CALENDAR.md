## Reserve topics

- What Cyber Essentials covers: the five technical controls.

## Review gates

- Weeks 3 to 12 must not be designed from working titles alone.
- Each week begins only after the source article or founder-approved brief
  exists.
- If a source changes, update the article and the social asset together.
- TikTok photo mode remains out of scope until two topics reach at least 1.5
  times the rolling median save-plus-share rate and the owner approves the
  additional channel.
