## Founder LinkedIn post

Write this as a second hook, not a repetition of page 1. Use short lines, one
idea per line, concrete numbers and three to five hashtags. Put the tracked URL
in the first comment.

## BrightCert repost commentary

Use a distinct company perspective. Do not copy the founder post.

## Instagram caption

Restate the practical result in searchable language. Use `Full guide in bio`
when the CTA is an article.

## Accessibility

Write one concise description for each page. Include all important text and
explain diagrams or comparisons without repeating decorative details.
