# BrightCert social infographic system

Owner: Muhammad Sohaib Roomi  
Primary channel: Muhammad's LinkedIn profile  
Secondary channel: BrightCert Instagram  
Cadence: one seven-page carousel and one derivative graphic per week

## Purpose

Turn each founder-reviewed Cyber Essentials guide into one useful, sourced
visual that a UK SME can understand without technical expertise.

This is a readiness education programme. It must never imply that BrightCert
issues Cyber Essentials certification or guarantees a pass.

## Editable source and exports

The editable source of truth is Canva. Until the Canva connection is restored,
the supplied PPTX files are the editable import source:

1. In Canva, choose **Create a design > Import file**.
2. Import `BrightCert-Social-Carousel-Master.pptx`.
3. Rename the imported design to `BrightCert Social Carousel Master`.
4. Lock the background, footer, logo and page-number elements.
5. Duplicate the master for each weekly post.
6. Import the Week 1 PPTX separately if the completed example is wanted.

Export each approved carousel as:

- PDF Standard for LinkedIn, with one consistent page size.
- PNG pages for Instagram, numbered in posting order.
- One 1080 x 1350 PNG derivative for a later reminder post.

LinkedIn accepts PDF document posts up to 100 MB and 300 pages. BrightCert
carousels are fixed at seven pages and should remain below 10 MB.

Keep critical content at least 60 px from every edge. Use at least 32 px for
body copy and 48 px for headings. Each page should communicate one main idea
and normally contain fewer than 30 words.

## Seven-page structure

1. A specific, standalone hook.
2. The direct answer or practical context.
3. The first actionable point.
4. The second actionable point.
5. The third actionable point.
6. A final value point, summary, comparison or evidence.
7. One transparent next step and the readiness disclaimer.

Every final page must include:

> BrightCert helps organisations prepare for Cyber Essentials. It does not
> issue certification.

## Weekly production

1. Copy the carousel brief template and select the source article.
2. Verify every non-trivial claim against an IASME or NCSC primary source.
3. Write the seven-page copy and platform captions.
4. Record the source beside each claim in the brief and in slide notes.
5. Obtain founder approval for requirements, prices and certification claims.
6. Duplicate the Canva master and populate it without changing locked elements.
7. Review every page at phone size and run the QA checklist.
8. Export the PDF, PNG sequence and derivative graphic.
9. Publish from Muhammad's LinkedIn profile.
10. Repost from BrightCert the following day with distinct company commentary.
11. Publish the PNG sequence on Instagram with page-level accessibility text.
12. Record results after 72 hours and seven days.

## Publishing and attribution

- Educational carousels have one CTA: read the complete guide.
- Product or comparison carousels have one CTA: start the free assessment.
- Put the LinkedIn URL in the first comment, not the post body.
- Use the Instagram bio link and state `Full guide in bio`.
- Do not combine `save`, `share`, `follow`, `read` and `start` requests.

Use:

```text
utm_source={linkedin|instagram}
utm_medium=organic-social
utm_campaign=ce_education_2026
utm_content={topic}-carousel
```

## Non-negotiable guardrails

- UK English and plain language.
- No copied Pinterest or competitor images.
- No fabricated customers, results, statistics or insider stories.
- No concealed advertising or promotional page disguised as neutral advice.
- No duplicate-account or platform-evasion tactics.
- No artificial urgency or unsupported scarcity.
- No verbatim reproduction of the official Cyber Essentials questionnaire.
- No user count until the underlying metric has been manually refreshed and
  approved.
- No reference to Monitor, CE Plus Pack or MSP Partner as purchasable products
  until their checkouts exist.
