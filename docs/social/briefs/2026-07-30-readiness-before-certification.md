## Founder LinkedIn post

Cyber Essentials renewal is not just annual admin.

It is a 12-month commercial deadline.

Government guidance says suppliers must recertify every 12 months to maintain a valid certificate.

Waiting until the certificate date leaves little room to:

• Confirm what is in scope
• Find gaps across the five controls
• Assign the fixes
• Check the work before applying

My practical rule is simple.

Start the readiness review 30 to 60 days before renewal.

This is not certification.

This is the preparation work that should happen before it.

BrightCert helps turn the first readiness check into a prioritised action plan.

It does not guarantee a pass.
