# BrightCert founder script

> **CANONICAL FOR `founding_pilot_2026`**

Use this wording for live introductions, calls, voice notes and replies where a
short spoken explanation of BrightCert is needed. It complements the approved
`sme-v1` and `msp-v1` emails; it does not replace their trigger, privacy,
opt-out or pre-send controls.

## Core introduction

> BrightCert helps UK businesses find out how ready they are for Cyber
> Essentials in around two hours. I guide them through a free readiness
> baseline and review the action plan with them, so they can see what evidence
> they already have and what to address next.
>
> BrightCert is a readiness service, not a Certification Body. It does not
> issue Cyber Essentials certification or guarantee that a business will pass.

If the person asks what happens after the baseline:

> The guided baseline and reviewed action plan are free. After that, the
> permanent report and evidence-workspace unlock is optional. It is £99
> including VAT with the existing £100 `FOUNDING10` discount from £199.

End with one question:

> Would a short outline be useful?

## Short objection answers

### “Will this certify us?”

> No. BrightCert helps you prepare and understand your likely gaps. Official
> Cyber Essentials certification comes from a Certification Body.

### “Does this guarantee we will pass?”

> No. It gives you a structured readiness view and action plan before you
> apply, but it cannot guarantee the Certification Body's decision.

### “Can everything be fixed in two hours?”

> No. Around two hours refers to finding out how ready you are by completing
> the baseline. The time needed to address any gaps depends on what the
> baseline finds.

### “What does it cost?”

> The guided baseline and reviewed action plan are free. The optional permanent
> report and evidence-workspace unlock is £99 including VAT with the existing
> £100 `FOUNDING10` discount from £199.

## Non-negotiable guardrails

- Never say BrightCert certifies, accredits, approves or guarantees a pass.
- Never say remediation, certification or full readiness is completed in two
  hours. The approved statement is: “Find out how ready you are in around two
  hours.”
- Never invent a deadline, cohort count, customer count, remaining place or
  other scarcity claim.
- Never describe the displayed Monitor, CE Plus Pack or MSP Partner plans as
  purchasable until their checkout paths are live and verified.
- Never quote stale user, customer, conversion or revenue figures.
- Use one CTA at a time. A reply is the primary outreach CTA; do not invent a
  public booking link.
- Keep the founding price exact: £99 including VAT through the existing £100
  `FOUNDING10` discount from £199.
- If a prospect asks for legal, certification or technical assurance outside
  this scope, answer honestly or follow up after checking; do not improvise.
